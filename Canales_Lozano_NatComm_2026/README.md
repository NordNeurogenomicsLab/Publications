### Data availability

Bulk RNA-seq data from total cortex and from nuclear, soluble/cytosolic, and synaptosomal fractions are available through GEO accession [GSE300997](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE300997).

Single-nucleus RNA-seq data, including raw FASTQ files and the integrated Seurat object, are available through GEO accession [GSE301511](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE301511).
